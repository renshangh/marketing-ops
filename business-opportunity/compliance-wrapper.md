# Compliance Wrapper

A compliance wrapper is the trust and governance layer around an AI device or platform that makes it safe, auditable, and enterprise-usable.

## Why it matters
If AI devices become as common as PCs, companies will still need to control:
- who accessed what
- what data left the device
- what actions the AI agent took
- whether actions were approved
- how to prove compliance later

## Core capabilities
- policy controls
- audit logs
- data loss prevention
- approval workflows
- identity and permissions
- retention and redaction
- admin dashboards

## Product angle
This becomes the enterprise envelope around the device, paired with platform support for:
- connectors
- setup
- admin lifecycle
- device management
- integration with existing enterprise systems

## Business value
Companies can adopt the device while keeping their security and compliance rules intact.
